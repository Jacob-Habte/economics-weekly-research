# Economics Weekly Research

Companion code, charts, and reproducibility notes for long-form Economics Weekly market analysis.

## Projects

| Project | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| [Private-Credit Market Analysis](private-credit-article/) | Public BDC performance, broader risk assets, high-yield spreads, drawdowns, volatility, and FSK relative to peers. |
| [Oil-Price Market Analysis](oil-price-article/) | WTI and Brent history, the $100 threshold, gasoline-price transmission, and a corporate sensitivity case study. |
| [Yen-Intervention Market Analysis](yen-intervention-article/) | Ten-year and twelve-month USD/JPY views supporting research into yen weakness and intervention. |

## Repository standards

- Each article is contained within its own project folder.
- Notebooks are divided into explanatory sections and use portable paths.
- Downloadable raw data stay outside version control.
- Selected final figures are retained for review.
- Credentials, private exports, local environments, and caches are excluded.
- Data sources, reproduction steps, assumptions, and limitations are documented beside each analysis.

## Disclaimer
